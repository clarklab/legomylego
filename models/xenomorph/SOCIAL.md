## Before posting

- If someone asks: the video is a render (Blender), and every piece in it is a real LEGO
  element in a colour it was made in. The cat is a real one too: the standing cat with the
  angry face, made in orange for one 2022 set, so look for it second-hand. Any LEGO standing
  cat fits.
- The two sounds were made for this video (models/xenomorph/audio/sfx.toml): they are not
  from any film.
