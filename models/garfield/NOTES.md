# Garfield

Garfield's head in 32 pieces, 4 cm wide and 3.5 cm tall: sleepy half-closed eyes under round
eyelids, a pink nose, two cheeks tipped in under it, jowls and temples on the sides and two
small ears at the back. zeldar's design, built the way zeldar's instructions build it, from the
owner's phone: the app's picture of it, its parts list and its 18 instruction pages.

## How it was read

**The pages and their order.** The five "All pages" screens hold 20 panels. The last screen
is scrolled to the end of the list, so its top row repeats the fourth screen's bottom row (the
two pairs of panels are the same to 0.3% of their pixels): 18 pages. Each page adds what the
one before shows plus its green pieces, and the green pieces of the 18 pages add up to the
parts list's 32 exactly, so no page is missing.

**Checking each page.** Each page was cut out of its screen and a render of the model built
up to that page was laid over it from the app's camera: orthographic, 45 degrees round from
the front, 30 up (the front at the lower left). Page 18 fits best there (98.5%, against 94-97%
5 degrees either way). Every page overlaps at 97-99%. The things only the overlays settled:

- **The face brackets hang under the 1 x 3 plate** (page 4): their bases' studs go into its
  end holes and their plates stand up in front of it (83% overlap if they stand on top of it,
  98% under it). The stud seen behind each bracket is the 1 x 3's own.
- **Page 6's two bricks are on their sides**: the front faces are 24 wide and 20 tall, one
  hollow side stud on the front and the top stud out to the side. Each sticks out half a plate
  past the face, flush with the side brackets of page 9 (their plates' outer faces).
- **Page 8 is two 1 x 1 plates** on the 1 x 3's middle stud (the green box is two plates tall,
  and eight 1 x 1 plates in the list need two here).
- **The curved slopes' two holes are a plate apart in depth**: one on the side brick's top stud,
  the other on the 1 x 1 plate stood on the side bracket's lower stud (page 11). Thick end up.
- **The cheese slopes** (page 13): of the four ways a cheese slope can go on a side stud, thin
  end down fits (98.8%; the others 95-96%).
- **The cheeks** (page 18) each sit on one stud, the side brick's, flat edge up, and are turned
  so their inner ends dip under the nose (which they would hit level). The tilt fits between
  12 and 18 degrees on both sides; 15 is used.
- **The eyes' print** is LDraw's 98138p2l (Rebrickable 98138pr9976, White, element 6434345): a
  black half eye with a white half pupil, the same as the hero picture, upright.

## What does not match the app exactly

- **Page 6 goes on at page 12.** zeldar puts the two bricks on their sides at page 6, but
  nothing holds them there: their top studs point out, their side studs to the front and their
  hollow undersides at the column's plain side. The curved slopes of page 12 are the first
  thing they clip into. So here each jowl is a small sub-assembly (one per side): the brick
  goes onto its curved slope's lower hole, and the two go on together at page 12's step, the
  curved slope's upper hole on the plate's stud (19 steps: 17 for the pages, 2 for the
  jowls). Every piece is where zeldar puts it. Built that way the checks pass and the video's
  planner finds a clear way in for every piece (`brickkit status --check`: clean).
- **The ears are Bright Light Orange.** LEGO never made the plate with a tooth (49668) in
  Orange; Bright Light Orange is its common colour nearest to it (Dark Orange was in one set).
- **The cheeks are Bright Light Orange.** The app draws them Medium Orange (#FFA70B, between
  the head's Orange and Bright Light Orange); the 1 x 2 half round tile never came in it.
- **The nose is Bright Pink.** The app draws Pink (#FC97AC); the round 1 x 1 tile never came in
  it, and Bright Pink is the nearest it did.

## Stability

About 10 g on a one-stud foot (the two 1 x 1 plates): it stands, and tips at 12.2 degrees
(the check wants 10), so no setting is needed. The side bricks and the cheeks each hang on one
stud.
