# Mario

A Quick Bricks model: 62 pieces, 13.3 cm tall, 6.4 cm wide across the arms and 4.8 cm deep. It
is a blocky Mario on a red 4 x 4 stand: a red cap with a peak, brown hair and a brown moustache
on a tan face, two black round eyes, blue overalls with two yellow buttons, a red shirt and
white gloves. The design is another builder's, JellsaTheBobJeffCreator, Rebrickable MOC-249307.
It was taken in from the designer's BrickLink Studio file with `brickkit import`: every part
sits where the designer put it. The build itself is in `brickkit/designs/mario.py`, which Luigi
shares (`models/luigi`). All 8 checks pass; the stability check's limit is lowered from 10
degrees to 7 (see below).

## What had to be worked out

The Studio file gives every part's place, so nothing about the shape was guessed. What follows
is what was changed or decided on the way in.

- **Which way he faces.** In the file he faces the other way. He was turned to face the front,
  so the stills and the video start on his face.
- **Colours by what they are.** The colours are palette roles (`shirt`, `stand`, `stand_top`,
  `overalls`, `skin`, `hair`, `glove`, `button`, `eye`) in a shared build,
  `brickkit/designs/mario.py`. `models/mario/design.py` only calls it, and Luigi is the same
  bricks with another palette.
- **The steps.** The designer's 29 small steps are grouped into 14, plus the arm. An arm's
  three bricks hang from the plate on top of them and stand on nothing, so the arm is a
  sub-assembly: it is built first and hung on whole, and nothing is loose between steps.
- **The two buttons.** In the file they are Tile Round 1 x 1 (98138) in Pearl Yellow. LEGO has
  no such tile: the catalogue has no element for it, and no Rebrickable or BrickLink colour
  behind LDraw's Pearl Yellow at all. They are Yellow here (element 6284577).
- **Does he stand?** Yes, but he tips easily backwards. His head is 4 studs deep and
  overhangs the stand by one stud at the back, and he stands on the stand's back three rows.
  He weighs about 83 g, and his centre of mass is 7.2 cm up and about 6 mm behind the middle
  of the stand, so he tips backwards at 8.2 degrees (the check wants at least 10). So
  `[checks.stability] min_tilt_deg = 7` in `model.toml`, with a comment. Nothing was added to
  steady him: the design is someone else's.
- **How the arms hold.** Only by the plate at the top of each, which lies on the chest's
  studs under the brick across the shoulders. That is the designer's way; the booklet's note
  says to press that brick down firmly.
- **Not tried in bricks.** The checks are on the computer model: 278 stud connections in one
  assembly, no overlaps, every piece with a clear way on. No physical build is recorded.
