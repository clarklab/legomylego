# The Thing

The Fantastic Four's Thing in 58 pieces, about 6 cm tall: a grumpy brow over two BrickHeadz
eyes, a cheese-slope nose, a black mouth, an ear on each side, corner-slope shoulders, blue
shorts, and two arms with clip hands that turn on their shoulder studs. zeldar's design, built
the way zeldar's instructions build it, from the owner's phone: the app's picture of it, its
parts list and its 36 instruction pages.

## How it was read

Each page was cut out of the screenshots and a render of the model, built up to the same step,
was laid over it from the app's camera: orthographic, 45 degrees round from the front, 30 up
(fitted on page 12: the two silhouettes overlap 98.6%). Pages 13 to 16 look from the front
left, pages 22 to 24 and 27 at the model lying on its back. Every page overlaps at 97-98%, and
the head pages were also scored on the head alone. Things only the overlays settled:

- **The head bracket is half a stud off centre.** The half pin is in its left stud hole (a
  stud hole, not the bar in the middle), so the face is centred a half stud right of the body.
- **The arms hang a little back** from straight down: about 20 degrees on the right, 15 on the
  left (page 20). They turn freely on their studs.
- **The brow and the top of the head are half a stud forward** of the side studs they stand
  on (pages 31 to 36: 0.97 against 0.95 a half stud either way). zeldar's side studs are hollow
  (87087's, and the 36841 brackets'): a 1 x 2 plate's underside tube goes into a hollow stud,
  so each brow plate sits centred on its side stud. The brow tiles hang centred on the
  brackets' studs the same way.
- **Two ears.** Page 25 adds a second ear bracket on the left end of the face (the app never
  shows that side), with a 1 x 1 plate over it, mirroring the right one.
- The eyes' print turns with the big white square at the top left; the nose's thick end is
  down; the three cheese slopes under the face have their thin ends against the chest.

## What does not match the app exactly

- **The half pin is Light Bluish Gray.** LEGO never made 4274 in Orange (the app draws it
  orange). It is inside the technic brick and behind the bracket: it does not show.
- **58 pieces, not 60.** The app's list has nine 1 x 1 plates; its pages put on seven. The
  other two show up on no page and leave no gap in any picture (spares, or pieces hidden in
  the app's file). The parts lists here have the 58 the pages use.
- **Page 3 is two steps** (both layers of legs at once cannot be pushed into place together).
- **The 1 x 2 tile's tube.** LDraw's 3069b (2010) leaves out the tube under its middle, so the
  engine could not hang zeldar's brow tiles on the hollow studs; `brickkit/data/shadow/parts/
  3069b.dat` adds it, as 3023b has it.

## Stability

The head hangs out in front of 1 x 2 feet: the centre of mass is 3.4 LDU inside their front
edge, so it stands but tips forward at about 2.6 degrees. That is zeldar's design: the check
is set to 2 degrees in model.toml, and the booklet says to stand him on a flat table. (1 x 3
feet with a stud of toe, tried in the first version, stand to 15 degrees, if the owner wants.)

## Parts and price

58 pieces in 24 lines, every one with a LEGO element ID. BrickLink $6.51, Pick a Brick $4.03
with 5 lines not sold there (2026-11-04, before shipping): the printed eyes, the corner slopes
(3045, rare in Orange: 7 sets, last in 2010), and the technic bricks in Orange come from
BrickLink. LDraw's 3660b is the mould LEGO sells as 3660 (`brickkit/data/part_map.json`).
