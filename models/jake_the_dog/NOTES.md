# Jake the Dog

An original 143-piece brick interpretation of Jake, made from the three character pictures
in the owner's inbox and designed as a companion to `finn_the_human`. Jake is 13.12 cm tall
beside Finn's 19.36 cm (68% of his height), with an eight-stud-wide, six-stud-deep rounded
body and the same one-stud round-brick limbs and pin-and-clip joints. The complete posed
model measures approximately 13.47 cm wide × 13.12 cm high × 8.07 cm deep, including arms,
nose and tail. The first delivery was the checked model, parts exports and still previews
(by `codex-jake`); the booklet, video, posts and site page followed the same day.

## What had to be worked out

- **Scale and silhouette:** the references vary with cartoon poses. We chose a short,
  broad capsule whose crown reaches roughly Finn's shoulder, keeping Finn's limb thickness
  and clean, solid-colour style. It is an interpretation, not an official LEGO set.
- **Hidden structure:** a hollow six-ring shell of macaroni corners, straight front/back
  bricks and side bricks is tied together by a five-plate floor and roof. Four dome corners
  and two curved slopes form the crown; the central brick supports the crown slopes.
  The hidden hip block spreads load into the belly. All structure is our own design.
- **Face:** large white round tiles over black dishes make the black eye outlines without
  printed parts. A forward-facing plate mounts the eyes, oval black nose and two round
  muzzle lobes. Smooth tiles cover the forehead and cheeks. The round jowls and a small
  black half-circle between them suggest the closed smile in `character.png`; we did not
  copy the open mouth from `standing.jpeg`. Parts intentionally
  simplify the organic jowls and leave some LEGO seams visible.
- **Ears and tail:** short yellow oval tiles suggest drooping ears; a yellow banana held in
  an open stud forms the small upturned tail. Their exact geometry is our interpretation
  of details partly hidden in the references.
- **Limbs and stance:** short cylindrical legs, forward-projecting rounded feet and jumper
  plates at the ankles keep the centre of mass over the soles. The arms use Finn's Technic
  shoulders and bar/clip elbows, posed outward with bent forearms rather than hands resting
  against the belly. Both shoulders and hips attach with real friction pins.
- **Build order:** face trim, smile and both muzzle supports go on before the eyes and nose, so
  each part has a clear insertion path. The body is built first, followed by leg and arm
  subassemblies; all 143 pieces occur exactly once in the model. The 39 construction steps
  remain in the MPD and design source for future instructions.
- **Confidence:** geometry, element availability, connectivity, collisions, insertion paths
  and standing stability are engine-checked. No collision exemptions, disabled checks or
  relaxed stability thresholds were added. This is digitally checked, not physically built.
  The shown pose is checked; a full joint-motion envelope has not been simulated.

## Parts and price

143 pieces in 39 part/colour lines, in Yellow, Black and White. Every line has a LEGO element
ID in the local catalog; no missing or rare part/colour combinations were reported. Complete
143-piece exports are `out/parts.csv` and `out/bricklink_wanted.xml`, with the
model in `out/jake_the_dog.mpd`. `out/pick_a_brick.csv` contains 139 pieces: the repository
marks the four black 2780 friction pins (element 4121715) unavailable from Pick a Brick and
omits them from that upload. Those four pins remain in the complete BOM and BrickLink list;
source them there or from existing parts. This is an explicit purchasing-channel difference,
not a missing model piece. The repository's offline estimate is **$6–$21 before
shipping**, not a live price or stock check. Nothing was placed in a cart or purchased.

## Verification and previews

All eight enabled model checks pass: real elements, connections, collisions, buildability,
stability, mechanism, electrics and technique. The model forms one connected assembly with
360 detected connections and no intersecting part pairs. All 144 tested insertions across
seven submodels pass. Estimated mass is 132 g; the minimum tipping angle is 11.4°, above
the engine's default threshold. Mechanism and electrics report no configured mechanisms or
electrics; this does not claim that the friction joints cannot be posed.

The repository test suite passed: **329 tests** (`.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q`,
2026-10-09, 325.73 seconds, final 143-piece revision). The separate assembly-plan audit is clean
(`brickkit status jake_the_dog --check`); this audit produces no video.

Still previews are in `out/renders/`: front, three-quarter, side and back. The companion
preview, `out/renders/with_finn.png`, shows Jake and the existing Finn on the same floor at their actual model scale.
The reports are `out/report.html` and `out/report.json`. A private viewer export under
`out/viewer/` provides the GLB, part list and step data without changing the public site.
