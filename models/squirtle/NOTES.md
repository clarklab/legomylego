# Squirtle

Our own playful brick interpretation of Squirtle: **42 pieces**, **20 instruction steps**, and **65.6 × 57.6 × 57.6 mm** (width × height × depth). The head is four studs wide. Broad blue flippers support a small body, a round tan belly, a brown domed shell with a tan rim, and a deliberately oversized curled tail made from three blue elbow tubes. The tail makes the overall model wider than its head. It stands on its feet, without a separate display base.

## Source and authorship

The owner supplied three character references in `inbox/starter-pokemon/`. All three were inspected and copied byte-for-byte into this model’s `reference/` on 2026-10-09:

- `Starters.webp`: a cartoon trio; Squirtle has a cyan body, cream belly, brown shell and curled tail.
- `download.webp`: toy-like starter figures; this informed the squat stance, wide head and short limbs.
- `images.jpeg`: a rendered character trio; Squirtle’s lively pose and prominent curl informed the exaggerated tail.

They stay local: they are character art of unknown source, so git ignores them. These are character references, not brick instructions. Their artists, original publication URLs and licensing were not supplied and remain unknown. The character belongs to the Pokémon franchise; this is an unofficial fan model. The brick construction, hidden support, part selection, proportions and assembly sequence are original work for this repository by Codex with the owner’s direction. No official LEGO model, downloaded brick design or designer’s instructions were copied.

## Interpretation and choices

- **Colour:** Medium Azure is a practical approximation of the reference cyan. Every selected part/colour combination has LEGO element IDs and passes the catalog’s real-element check without rare-part warnings. The three blue 25214 elbows occur in four catalogued sets; they are the least widespread blue shape used here.
- **Proportions:** The four-stud head and exaggerated curl favour recognition and humour within the piece limit. The geometric head, simple round belly and one-piece flippers simplify the rounded reference anatomy; the references do not specify dimensions or a LEGO construction.
- **Eyes and expression:** The eyes are real printed white round tiles (`98138p07`, BrickLink `98138pb007`, Rebrickable `98138pr0008`), with modeled black pupils. Their black-and-white print substitutes for the detailed coloured eyes in the references. A half-round black tile below the eyes gives a small smile.
- **Shell:** A smaller brown dish sits on the larger tan dish with a one-plate spacer. The spacer is necessary: nesting the dishes directly would overlap their rims. The round layered shell suggests the pale rim without inventing painted shell markings.
- **Tail:** Three 90-degree elbow tubes form an open curl. Each notched two-stud axle fits two opposing 20-LDU sockets; the red axles are internal. The tail begins on a short plate behind the positive-X heel, rather than inside the body. This attachment and the oversized curl are deliberate stylisations. The last tube’s socket is left open, like the centre of a curl.
- **Clearance and sequence:** The hips are raised one plate above the curved toes; the tail support is fitted before the arms cover its insertion path. These are construction decisions, not details recoverable from the source art.
- **Limits:** The checks validate the digital model, not a physical prototype or grip strength after repeated handling. Front, three-quarter, side and back still previews were inspected against all three references, along with the same-scale trio preview. The open curl and layered shell are intentional; no visible floating or intersecting parts remain.

## Checks

- `brickkit all squirtle`: all eight checks pass, with none switched off and no contact
  allowed. 115 connections (108 stud, 6 axle, 1 pin), one assembly, no overlaps, every piece has a clear way on. It weighs
  about 36 g and tips at 19.5 degrees.
- `brickkit status squirtle --check`: the video's build plan is clean (no piece passes through
  another, each lands on something).
- The booklet follows the house rules (docs/new-model.md): every piece shows in its step's
  pictures, no step is one piece by itself, nothing stands loose from one step to the next,
  and no step adds more than about four things.
- Checked on a computer only. Nobody has built it with real bricks yet.
