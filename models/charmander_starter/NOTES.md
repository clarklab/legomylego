# Charmander Starter

An original 37-piece Charmander with a four-stud-wide head, friendly printed eyes, a rounded tan belly, short reaching arms and a curved orange tail with a real translucent flame. The complete model is approximately **61.4 mm wide × 65.0 mm tall × 68.2 mm deep**, including the tail and flame; the head itself is 32 mm wide. It uses 16 short assembly steps, adding one to five pieces per step. It faces -Z and stands on its own feet.

## What had to be worked out

- **Original construction, high confidence:** all geometry, concealed supports and assembly order were designed here. The three references are consistent about the orange body, pale belly, big eyes, short arms, clawed feet and flame-tipped tail. They do not prescribe a LEGO inventory.
- **Intentional simplification:** the head is a rounded block rather than a sphere; its two printed eye tiles have black pupils and white glints rather than custom blue irises. A black tile suggests an open mouth without fangs or a custom mouth print. White grille ridges suggest claws within the small piece budget.
- **Belly:** Tan is the readily available pale warm colour used for the rounded chest; the reference yellow / cream was interpreted rather than sampled exactly.
- **Tail and flame:** genuine Orange 40379 is tilted 40° around its rear-facing bar. At the tail tip, two standard open-stud round plates form a coaxial collar: the lower one grips the tail's 3.18 mm bar and the upper one receives the flame's 3.18 mm bar. The collar and flame are all Trans-Orange. The 16° tip bend comes from the part's actual snap geometry. All three joints are detected and checked; there are no connection overrides, exemptions or collision allowances.
- **Build order:** the feet go down with the hips that join them, and the tail goes on before the head: the video's last close-up is then the eyes, and it ends on the face (with the flame as the last piece it ended behind it).
- **Checks versus physical review:** the complete digital model has been checked by the repository's default checks and assembly planner. Front, three-quarter, side and back stills were inspected against the supplied references, along with a same-scale preview of the trio. It has not been hand built.
- **Preview limitation:** the current still renderer makes the Trans-Orange flame and collar appear almost clear against its pale background. Their source colours and parts exports remain correctly Trans-Orange; the preview is not an exact colour proof for transparent pieces.

## Parts and price

37 physical pieces in 20 part/colour lines. Every line has a real LEGO element ID; there are no unknown parts, approximation substitutions or catalogue rarity warnings. The printed eyes are the exact LDraw `98138p2j` mapping to Rebrickable `98138pr9981`, BrickLink `98138pb367`, element 6431716. The Orange curved tail is element 6194852, the translucent open-stud plates are element 6510162, and the flame is element 6167741.

The repository's rough estimate is **USD $2–$6 before shipping**, based on generic part bands rather than a live quote. `out/parts.csv`, `out/pick_a_brick.csv`, `out/bricklink_wanted.xml` and `out/price_estimate.md` are exported. Nothing was ordered or added to a cart.

## Checks

- `brickkit all charmander_starter`: all eight checks pass, with none switched off and no contact
  allowed. 80 connections (77 stud, 2 bar, 1 pin), one assembly, no overlaps, every piece has a clear way on. It weighs
  about 28 g and tips at 16.8 degrees.
- `brickkit status charmander_starter --check`: the video's build plan is clean (no piece passes through
  another, each lands on something).
- The booklet follows the house rules (docs/new-model.md): every piece shows in its step's
  pictures, no step is one piece by itself, nothing stands loose from one step to the next,
  and no step adds more than about four things.
- Checked on a computer only. Nobody has built it with real bricks yet.
