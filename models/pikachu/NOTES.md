# Surprised Pikachu

A Quick Bricks model: the head from the "surprised Pikachu" picture, 50 pieces. The head is
7 studs wide and 5 tall (5.6 x 4.2 cm) and 2.7 cm deep; with the ears it is 9 cm across and
6 cm tall. It has wide eyes, red cheeks, a small black nose over an open pink mouth, and two
pointed ears with black tips that swing on clips. The design is someone else's: it was taken
in from a designer's 9-page instructions.

`design.py` has one step per page, with one change in the ears (see *What had to be worked
out*). All 8 checks pass; one part is rare in yellow (see *Parts and price*).

## Source

- **From:** a set of instructions as a PDF, `pikachu-SurprisedFace.pdf`, 9 pages, dated
  17 January 2021. Each page is one numbered step: a callout of that step's parts (a drawing
  and a count each, no names, numbers or colours) and a drawing of the build so far with the
  new parts outlined in red. Pages 1 to 3 and 6 are drawn from the front; pages 4, 5 and 7
  to 9 from behind. Page 9 is the last step and the finished model. There is no parts list.
- **Designer / credit:** not known. No page names a designer and the file name has no MOC
  number. The PDF's own author field (its properties, not its pages) does hold a name; it has
  not been confirmed as the designer's, so it is not repeated here. Check it and credit the
  designer before this is published.
- **Saved in `reference/`:** nothing, on purpose. The repo is public and these are the
  designer's instructions, so neither the PDF nor its page pictures are copied in. The
  originals are in `inbox/pikachu/` (the PDF, and `pages/page_01.png` to `page_09.png`),
  which is local only.
- **Taken in:** 8 October 2026 by pika-agent (stages 1 to 3).

## What had to be worked out

The pages give drawings only: no part names, numbers or colours. Every part was named from
its drawing, then placed where the drawings put it. To check the result, the model was
drawn with the pages' own camera and laid over pages 6 (front) and 9 (back); the two line up
piece for piece.

Sure:

- **The curved slopes straddle edges.** The 2 x 1, 2 x 2 and 3 x 1 curved slopes have a step
  underneath: the thick end sits one plate higher than the thin end. Each one here has its
  thick end on a plate's studs and its thin end hanging over that plate's edge. That is why
  page 1 has a 1 x 2 plate under the top slopes, and why the face is flush. The callouts show
  the step, and the drawings only fit this way.
- **It is lopsided on purpose.** The picture it copies shows him three-quarters on. One side
  of the head has the big wedge and a square jaw, the other a cut corner; the mouth is round
  on one side only.
- **Left and right parts.** The wedge is the Right one (43720) because its wide end is down.
  The notched slope is the Right one (29119) because its cut corner is out and down.
- **The ears.** Each is a stack on its clip plate: a plate under it, a plate over its outer
  stud, two curved slopes back to back on top (yellow, then black) and an inverted one under
  the tip. Read from pages 8 and 9 and confirmed by every stud meeting a hole.

Less sure:

- **The eyes** (page 2). The drawings show a black round tile with a white circle a little
  over half its width, off centre. Two real prints fit that description. 98138pr0076 has a
  circle that size and is used here; it was in 5 sets, the last in 2020. 98138pr0107 has a
  much smaller dot and is far more common (100 sets); it would do if the first is hard to
  find. Fairly sure.
- **The mouth's colour** (page 6). The drawings' pink is closest to Coral, and both mouth
  parts exist in Coral. Bright Pink would also work. Fairly sure.
- **The cheeks** (page 3) are drawn with a flat top, so they are round tiles (98138), not
  round plates.
- **The top slopes** (page 1) are 3 x 1 (50950), not 4 x 1: the two together are six studs
  long, the width of the face. Sure of the length.
- **The jumper** (page 2). The drawing cannot tell the two moulds apart. 15573, the current
  one, is used.
- **The ears' angle.** About 36 degrees above level, measured off pages 7 to 9, give or take
  3. They are on clips, so they can be set anywhere.

Changed from the plans:

- **The plate under each ear goes on first.** Page 7 clips the bare clip plates to the
  handles and page 8 presses a 1 x 2 plate up under each. With the ear on its hinge there
  are 18 LDU (7 mm) between it and the top of the jaw. That is enough by hand, and the ear
  swings out of the way, but brickkit's checks and the video want 24 LDU of clear run for a
  piece. So `design.py` makes each ear's base first (`ear_base`: the clip plate with its
  plate under it) and clips the two on at the start of step 8. Step 7 is then the 2 x 2
  plate and the two handles only. The pieces and where they end up are the same.
- **Three hints for the order.** The bridge of the nose goes on before the nose, the
  mouth's plate before its tiles, and the 2 x 2 plate of page 7 comes up from below
  (`insert=` in `design.py`).

Worth knowing:

- Each of the two top slopes, the notched slope and the 2 x 1 slope beside it is held by a
  single stud, as drawn.
- The Technic brick's holes are not used by anything.

## Parts and price

- 50 pieces in 26 lines; every part has a LEGO element ID, and the `real_elements` check
  finds nothing that is not real.
- One warning: the wedge, 43720 in Yellow, is rare (3 sets, the last in 2005). It is the
  part the plans show. Expect to buy it on BrickLink.
- `out/pick_a_brick.csv` and `out/bricklink_wanted.xml` are ready to upload.
- The rough estimate is $2-$6 (`out/price_estimate.md`). Prices have not been checked live.

## Quick Bricks

- `[quick]` in `model.toml`: a blue cutting mat in front of the bookshelf, in daylight,
  14 seconds, clicks only. Close-ups are asked for on the eyes, the mouth and an ear's black
  tip (tags `eyes`, `mouth`, `ear_tip`).
- With 50 pieces the parts fly in; they do not start laid out.
- `brickkit status pikachu --check` says the build plan is clean. Each ear's base is put
  together beside the head and clipped on as one. The planner makes the video 15 seconds,
  not 14, to fit the lifts and joins.
- No booklet or video has been made, and `collection` is not set.
