# Onkey

A Quick Bricks model: Onkey, a little brick-built monkey head with long arms lying out to the
sides, in 79 pieces. It is 9.6 cm from hand to hand, 4.2 cm deep and 6.4 cm tall to the tip of
its tuft: a reddish brown head with a rounded top and a tuft, two googly eyes, a tan muzzle
whose jaw drops, tan ears with a darker inner ear, and a rounded hand on the end of each arm.
It is theroldangrace's design, taken in from a 21-second build video on Instagram (no plans, no
parts list) and modelled our own way wherever the video doesn't show how it goes together. All
8 checks pass.

## What had to be worked out

- **The size.** The face in the video is five studs across, the muzzle four, centred: half a
  stud off the face's grid, and the eyes (centred 1.5 studs either side of the middle) are
  too. Ours is a core four studs wide and four deep: on its grid the muzzle, the eyes and the
  nose all line up, with no jumpers, and the eyes reach just past the sides of the face with
  the ears right behind them, as in the video. (A first try had a 2 x 3 tile on each side of
  the face to make it five studs wide; beside the video it looked boxed in.) The proportions
  were measured off the frames: muzzle 4 studs, eyes 1.4 studs, face a little under 5.
- **The eyes.** In the frames the white of an eye is about 1.4 studs across and the black
  three quarters of that, and the black has its own edge: a separate piece. That is a white
  Cone 1.5 x 1.5 x 2/3 (33492) with a black Tile Round 1 x 1 on its top stud: 27 and 20 LDU,
  as measured. They go on the studs of the eye row's end bricks, bricks with a stud on the
  front.
- **The muzzle.** Two curved 4 x 1 bricks, one above the line and one below. The upper lip is
  93273 (Brick Curved 4 x 1 Double): only its two end holes are at its foot, the middle two
  are a plate higher, so it sits over a tan 1 x 2 plate that fits up inside it, on a tan 2 x 3
  plate whose front row is the tan band under the lip (as in the video: the upper half is a
  plate taller than the lower). 79756 (the other 1 x 4 x 2/3 curved brick) curves across its
  width, not at its ends, and looked like a bar.
- **The jaw, and how it drops.** The video shows the jaw drop but not what it hangs on. A
  curved brick upside down can only be held from above by studs pointing down, and above it
  is the lip's own plate. So our jaw is two Brick Curved Inverted 2 x 1 (24201), thin ends
  out, which have their studs on top, under two tan 1 x 3 tiles; the tiles are smooth on top,
  so the upper lip rests on them without holding them. Each 24201's top steps down a plate at
  its thick end, with a stud there: a tan 1 x 1 tile fills each step. The tiles stand on two
  columns, two 1 x 1 plates on a plate with a clip, and each clip holds the bar of a plate with
  a handle on the back row, a plate below the jaw's foot. So the jaw swings forward and down
  about that bar and never back into the face: with the bar higher, the foot of the jaw swung
  back into the head; with it at the jaw's own height, the jaw's foot did. It holds wherever it
  is put (clip and bar is a friction hinge). The model opens it 7 degrees, about 6 mm at the
  front, as in the video; the checks sweep it there and back. The clip's mouth faces away
  from its plate, so it goes on from the front, mouth first, before the plates in front of it
  (whose studs would be in its way).
- **The nose.** A reddish brown round 1 x 1 tile on a half pin (4274) in a 1 x 2 Technic brick
  between the eyes: the hole is in the middle of the brick, where no stud is. A jumper there
  fouled the eyes' cones. The video's nose is a square brick with a dark slot under it; ours
  is round and has no slot.
- **The ears.** Each is a D, two studs out and four tall: two tan macaroni tiles (27925), round
  sides out, with a Medium Nougat half circle tile (1748) in the hole they leave, flat side
  in: the darker inner ear. They face the front, right beside the face and a stud behind it,
  on two tall bricks with studs on one side stacked on a 1 x 2 plate across the arm (a 1 x 1
  tile covers its front stud). The half circle bridges the two tall bricks. The video's ears
  are a little smaller (about three studs tall); ours are four, from the muzzle to the
  forehead. A first try stood them at the back of the head, behind the side tiles: too far
  back.
- **The arms.** 1 x 6 plates under the front row, their inner two studs under the head, a
  rounded 1 x 1 brick (49307) for the forearm, and the ear's plate on the stud between. The video's hands are domes of two quarter
  domes (1871); those are a Standard part on Pick a Brick in Reddish Brown, so ours are a
  1 x 2 brick with a 1 x 2 double-curved brick on top: a rounded mitten, and the whole kit
  stays in the Bestseller range.
- **The top.** Curved 1 x 2 x 1 bricks (37352) all round the edge, each curving down to the
  outside, and in the middle two plates and a 2 x 2 jumper for the tuft: a round 1 x 1 plate
  and a rounded 1 x 1 brick (49307).
- **Not done:** the dark slot under the nose; the hidden Dark Blue piece (ours has no part in
  another colour inside but two tan plates under the top and the grey half pin).
