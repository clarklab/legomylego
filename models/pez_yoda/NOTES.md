# Yoda PEZ

A Quick Bricks model: 60 pieces, 10.6 cm tall, about the size of a real PEZ dispenser. It is a
sand green Yoda head (two printed eyes, a brow, a round nose, two long ears) on a tan stem with
a small foot, and the head tips back on a click hinge to show an orange sweet, as a real one
does. It is 5.6 cm from ear tip to ear tip, and the foot is 1.6 cm by 3.2 cm. It is our own
design, made from pictures of a real dispenser, and one of three PEZ models on the same stem
(`brickkit/designs/pez.py`; the others are `pez_stormtrooper` and `pez_darth_vader`). All 8
checks pass; the stability check's limit is lowered from 10 degrees to 6 (see below).

## What had to be worked out

Everything: the pictures show a moulded plastic toy, and the bricks are ours. These are the
choices, and what is not like the real thing.

- **The size.** A real dispenser is about 10.5 cm tall with its head. That is the figure the
  design was made to; the pictures carry no scale and no dispenser was measured. Ours is 264
  LDU: 10.6 cm. The foot is a 2 x 4 plate, 1.6 cm by 3.2 cm, long from front to back. The stem
  is one stud wide and two deep and, with its tiles, 14.4 mm by 16 mm and 6.4 cm tall. The
  head is three studs square at its core, 2.9 cm tall, 3 cm wide over the cheeks and 5.6 cm
  over the ears.
- **The stem.** Four rounds of two 1 x 2 plates and two bricks with a stud on each side
  (47905), and two 2 x 4 tiles standing on those studs each side for the smooth sides. Two
  jumper plates centre the one-stud stem on the two-stud foot. It is solid: there is no
  magazine and no spring.
- **The sweet.** One 1 x 2 tile on top of the stem, which does not come out. The head rests
  on it when shut, and it shows when the head is tipped back. Its colour (Bright Light
  Orange) is our choice; the pictures show no sweets.
- **The hinge.** Two locking hinge plates: the one with two fingers (54657) flat on top of the
  stem, its fingers past the back, and the one with one finger (44301b) upright on the back of
  the head, finger down. They click every 22.5 degrees, and the design tips the head back
  three clicks: 67.5 degrees. The mechanism check sweeps 24 poses of it and finds nothing in
  the way. The head is held by the hinge alone; the sweet under it is a tile and grips
  nothing.
- **Which hinge plates, and their colour.** The older moulds of the same pair are dated: in
  Tan, 44301a was last in a set in 2005 and 44302a in 2006. 54657 is in Tan sets up to 2026
  (15 sets). 44301b in Tan is in only 3 sets, the last in 2025: the scarcest line of the three
  PEZ models. `real_elements` does not flag it (it calls a part rare under 3 sets). The
  head's hinge plate is Tan like the stem, on the back of a green head: the catalogue has no
  locking hinge plate of this kind in Sand Green.
- **The head's core.** Eight tall bricks with two studs on one side (32952) in a ring, hollow
  in the middle, tied by the plates on top. Three face the front and carry the face, two face
  out on each side, and one faces back for the hinge. Each side's two bricks are joined first
  by the tile on their studs (a cheek), so nothing stands loose while the head is built.
- **What in the face stands for what.** The eyes are two white round tiles printed with a
  black eye and two glints (98138p2j; BrickLink 98138pb367), turned so the glints are at the
  top. The cheese slope between them, high edge up, is the brow. The round plate below, its
  stud forward, is the nose. Each ear is a 1 x 3 plate with a curved slope on its outer two
  studs, thin end out for the tip.
- **The colours.** Sand Green stands for the green of the head, which is more olive in one
  picture and paler in the other. Tan is the stem's own colour in the pictures.
- **What could not be done at this size.** In the pictures the ears come out of the sides of
  the head, about level with the eyes. Here they come out of the top of the head, above the
  eyes, and stick straight out. The real eyes are small under heavy lids; the printed tiles
  are big and round, so he looks wider awake than the real one. There is no mouth and there
  are no wrinkles: the face is two eyes, a brow and a nose. The head is a box with a low
  rounded top. The stem's raised letters are left out, and the foot is two plates thick where
  the real one is thin.
- **How the ears hold.** Each ear's plate is on one stud of the head, the other two studs
  sticking out. The 1 x 3 plates in front of it and behind it keep it from turning, and
  nothing presses it down from above: the slope over that stud has its cut-out there (next).
  It passes the checks, but it is the weakest join in the model.
- **The curved slopes' cut-outs.** A curved slope (11477) has a cut-out one plate high under
  its thick end. So each of the six holds by the one stud under its thin end, and the stud
  under its thick end shows in the cut-out: a small notch in the middle of each side of the
  head's top and one at the root of each ear (`out/renders/close_head.png`).
- **Does it stand?** Like the real thing, only just: it is tall on a small foot. It weighs
  about 32 g and tips to the side at 7.2 degrees, the foot being two studs wide (the check
  wants at least 10). So `[checks.stability] min_tilt_deg = 6` in `model.toml`, with a
  comment.
- **Not tried in bricks.** The checks are on the computer model: 97 connections (96 stud, 1
  hinge) in one assembly, no overlaps, every piece with a clear way on. No physical build is
  recorded, so whether the hinge holds the head up when it is tipped back, and how well the
  ears stay on, is not known.

## Parts and price

- 60 pieces in 19 lines. Every line has a LEGO element ID; none is flagged rare.
  `out/parts.csv`, `out/pick_a_brick.csv` and `out/bricklink_wanted.xml` are ready to upload.
- The rough estimate is $2 to $7, new parts on BrickLink, before shipping. It is not a live
  price.
- By colour: 30 Sand Green, 27 Tan, 2 White (the eyes), 1 Bright Light Orange. The lines in
  the fewest sets are the Tan hinge plates (44301b in 3 sets, 54657 in 15), the printed eye
  (24 sets) and the Sand Green tall brick (32952, 30 sets): the ones to check first when
  ordering.

## Quick Bricks

- `[quick]` in `model.toml`: the blue LEGO floor (`lego_blue`: sand green stands out on blue)
  in the bookshelf room, day light, 18 seconds, clicks only.
- `highlight = ["eyes", "ears", "sweet"]`, three close-ups: the sweet as it goes on the stem,
  then an eye and an ear as the head is built.
- `flex = 2.8`: in the last seconds before the cut the head tips back on its hinge (the
  mechanism's own pose, eased), holds while the sweet shows, and shuts again, so the video
  ends on the face.
- With 60 pieces the parts come in from off frame (the opening with the parts laid out is for
  models under 25).
- The video's build plan is clean, audited the way `brickkit status pez_yoda --check` does it:
  no piece passes through another and each lands on something.
- `out/renders_open/three_quarter.png` is a still with the head tipped back and the sweet
  showing.
- The final video was not rendered when this was written; `brickkit status` shows where it is.
  `collection = "quick_bricks"` is not set: it is not on the site.
