head with a printed eye on each side, a jumper for a blowhole, a long back that slopes down to
the water behind, and two cheese-slope flukes coming up out of a blue 4 x 12 plate.

## What had to be worked out

- **The curved slopes' stepped underside.** 15068's high end is hollowed a plate higher
  underneath than its low end (LDraw has it so, and pages 7 and 9 draw the step in the red
  outline). That is how each sits on the two stacked plates (16 high) and a brick (24 high) at
  once. Certain: the overlays and the connections check agree.
- **Where everything is along the plate**, read from the plate's studs left uncovered on each
  page and settled by the overlays: one row of sea in front of the snout, three behind the whale,
  the tail in the middle one of those, centred across.
- **Which way the slopes face.** The 85984 and the 15068 at the back slope down to the tail; the
  tail's cheese slopes slope towards each other (their high, upright backs outside), as page 10
  draws and the picture shows.
- **The eyes' print.** The app's list says only 98138 and gives no colours. Its picture shows a
  white round tile printed with a black dome over a lid line and a white pupil in the middle: in
  LDraw, 98138p2g, "Eye Half Closed and Centred Pupil" (Rebrickable 98138pr0082, BrickLink
  98138pb183, element 6329607 in White, 51 sets). The picture and page 11 have it upside down
  from how LEGO names it: the lid's line along the bottom, the dome up; so it is here. (98138pr0027
  is the same print with the pupil off to one side: the picture's is centred.)
- **Colours, from the picture:** the plate is Blue (its median colour, 1/87/153, is LEGO Blue,
  not Dark Azure); everything else is White; the eyes' print black.

## What does not match the app exactly

Nothing in the construction. Every piece is the app's part number, in the place and way round
the pages show; one step here per page. The only addition is the eye print's number (above),
which the app's list leaves off.
