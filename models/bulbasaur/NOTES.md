# Bulbasaur

An original 48-piece LEGO interpretation of Bulbasaur from Pokémon, made from the owner's
three starter-character pictures. It has a low teal body on four paws, a four-stud-wide
head with large red eyes, pointed ears, dark green spots and an oversized round bulb with
three bright green leaves. The booklet has 19 steps, none adding more than 5 things. The complete geometry
measures 38.4 mm wide × 68.6 mm high × 60.4 mm deep; the body itself is four studs wide.

## Source

- **From:** three owner-supplied character pictures in `inbox/starter-pokemon/`, inspected
  on 2026-10-09. These are character references, not LEGO instructions or part inventories.
- **Design:** original brick model by this project, inspired by Bulbasaur from Pokémon.
  No LEGO build was copied. The reference artists/photographers are unknown; the owner did
  not supply original URLs or credits.
- **Saved in `reference/`:** `Starters.webp` (illustrated Kanto starter trio), `download.webp`
  (toy versions of the trio), and `images.jpeg` (angular papercraft interpretations). The
  originals remain in the inbox. They are character art of unknown source, so they stay local: git ignores them.
- **Taken in:** 2026-10-09 by `codex-starter-bulba`.

## What had to be worked out

- **Silhouette and proportions:** the teal quadruped, red eyes, pointed ears and green bulb
  are visible in all three references. The compact, deliberately chunky LEGO proportions
  and exact dimensions are our own interpretation, with the bulb as the visual focal point.
- **Hidden structure and build order:** all connections were designed here. Four white
  1×2 soles support four teal legs; a single 4×6 plate bridges them. Two body bricks and the
  central infill support the head and bulb. All 48 pieces belong to one connected assembly.
- **Face:** two white round jumpers carry dark red round-tile pupils. Side-stud bricks attach
  the eyes and a black half-circle tile to the front. The wide eyes and simple curved grin
  are a playful abstraction, not a literal tracing of the reference expressions.
- **Bulb:** a round 4×4 plate and brick support four curved corner bricks. A central jumper
  carries the three-leaf element. This gives a recognizably rounded bulb without requiring
  the rare older large-cone part considered during design. The leaves are Bright Green;
  this mould does not have a verified Lime combination in the repository catalog.
- **Markings and paws:** two dark green round flank spots and a dark green forehead slope
  suggest the irregular markings; white sole plates suggest the tiny claws. These simple
  substitutions keep the build under 60 pieces and avoid stickers or custom printed parts.
- **Confidence:** part identities, colours and attachment geometry are checked digitally.
  The likeness is a creative interpretation. This has not been physically test-built.

## Parts and price

48 pieces across 21 part/colour lines. Every line has a real LEGO element ID; none is flagged
rare or approximate, and no substitute print, custom piece or additional hardware is used.
The rough catalog estimate is US$2–$7, excluding shipping and actual seller stock.
`out/parts.csv`, `out/pick_a_brick.csv`, `out/bricklink_wanted.xml` and the price files are
ready for reuse.

## Checks

- `brickkit all bulbasaur`: all eight checks pass, with none switched off and no contact
  allowed. 117 stud connections, one assembly, no overlaps, every piece has a clear way on. It weighs
  about 43 g and tips at 28.4 degrees.
- `brickkit status bulbasaur --check`: the video's build plan is clean (no piece passes through
  another, each lands on something).
- The booklet follows the house rules (docs/new-model.md): every piece shows in its step's
  pictures, no step is one piece by itself, nothing stands loose from one step to the next,
  and no step adds more than about four things.
- Checked on a computer only. Nobody has built it with real bricks yet.
